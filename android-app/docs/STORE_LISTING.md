## Full description

```
GROW EMPIRE — SHOCKER OWNZ
Grow Like You Own The Show.

A deep cannabis cultivation tycoon simulation. Start with a single seed
and build a full grow empire: hunt rare phenotypes, preserve elite keepers,
breed custom genetics, run your dispensary, automate your facilities, and
climb from Street Rookie to Living Legend.

CULTIVATION SIMULATION
• Run interactive grow rooms across 9 facility tiers — water, feed, train
  and monitor every plant through 10 visual growth stages.
• Manage environment: lights, HVAC, humidity, CO₂ and sensors.

PHENOTYPE HUNTING & KEEPER VAULT
• Every seed grows a unique phenotype with hidden traits to discover.
• Hunt 15 traits across 4 rarity tiers, including 8 legendary expressions.
• Mark elite finds as Keepers and preserve them in your Keeper Vault.

BREEDING LAB
• Cross strains through F1–F5, backcross and S1 generations with real
  trait inheritance and rare mutations.
• Take clones that preserve genetics exactly. Track every lineage.

DISPENSARY & BUSINESS TYCOON
• Serve 5+ trait-based buyers, sign contracts, manage shopping carts,
  regulars and reviews in a living dynamic market.
• Process and package product, hire and train staff, upgrade 10 buildings,
  claim 6 territories, and outmaneuver 4 AI competitors.

PROGRESSION
• 114 missions including timed challenges, 13 ranks with real unlocks,
  achievements, competitions with trophies, and daily events & news.
• Rank-gated automation: auto-water, auto-feed and full facility systems
  with an upgradeable tech tree.

PROJECT 0
• The soul of the empire: preservation over profit. Chase the Project 0
  legacy — IT'S NEVER ABOUT THE MONEY. ONLY THE GENETICS.

Grow Empire is a fictional simulation game. It is entertainment only and
does not provide medical, legal, or horticultural advice. All data is
stored locally on your device.
```
