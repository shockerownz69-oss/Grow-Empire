# PRIVACY POLICY — Grow Empire (Shocker OwnZ)

**Effective date:** with the release of Grow Empire v1.0.0
**App:** Grow Empire · **Developer:** Shocker OwnZ
**Contact:** via the Google Play store listing for this app.

## The short version

Grow Empire stores **all of your game data locally on your device only**.
We do not operate servers for this game, and version 1.0.0 collects,
transmits, and shares **no personal data whatsoever**.

## Data we collect

**None.** Specifically, Grow Empire v1.0.0 contains:

- **No analytics** — nothing about how you play leaves your device.
- **No crash reporting** — crash data is never transmitted.
- **No advertising SDKs** — no ad identifiers are accessed.
- **No account servers** — "Grow Empire ID" profiles are local device
  profiles protected by a device-local PIN-style password. They are not
  cloud accounts and cannot be recovered if you lose the device or clear
  app data.
- **No cloud saves** in v1.0.0 — progress lives in on-device storage only.
  Uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently erases progress.

## Permissions

- **Internet** — declared for future features. The game is bundled with the
  app and runs fully offline; v1.0.0 makes no network requests.
- No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or notification permissions
  are requested in v1.0.0.

## Data safety (Google Play)

For the Play Data safety section: **no data collected, no data shared**.

## Children

Grow Empire is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

## Changes

If analytics, crash reporting, authentication, cloud saves, or any other
networked service is added in a future version, this policy will be updated
**before** that version ships, and the Data safety section will be amended
to describe exactly what is collected and why.

## Your control

Because no data leaves your device, there is nothing to request, export, or
delete from our side. To erase everything, uninstall the app or clear its
storage in Android Settings.
